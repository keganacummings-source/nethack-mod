PLAYER CLASS LORE — the sixteen custom roles
============================================


1) ALCHEMIST  (filecode Alc)
   Home: the Crucible Laboratories
   Gods: Hermes, Isis, Set
   Play: Potion-heavy support/chaos. Starts with healing, acid, oil,
         sickness, and mystery potions. Soft defenses, high Int.
   Body: Healer chassis.

2) HEXER  (Hex)
   Home: the Coven of Ashen Willows
   Gods: Hecate, Orcus, Nyarlathotep
   Play: Debuff caster. Fear, confuse, sleep books; cloak of protection.
         Attrition and control over raw damage. Wis/Int focused.
   Body: Wizard chassis.

3) CORSAIR  (Cor)
   Home: Port Blackwake
   Gods: Poseidon, Ran, Davy Jones
   Play: Mobile raider. Scimitar + spare daggers, boots, booze, gold.
         Aggressive melee/throw hybrid with loot focus.
   Body: Rogue chassis.

4) GEOMANCER  (Geo)
   Home: the Deeproot Catacombs
   Gods: Geb, Gaia, Typhon
   Play: Terrain controller. Pick-axe, dig + stone-to-flesh books,
         rocks, touchstone. Break walls, reshape fights.
   Body: Archeologist chassis.

5) WARDEN  (War)
   Home: the Verdant Bastion
   Gods: Artemis, Silvanus, Pan
   Play: Guardian fighter. Sword + bow, shield, protection spell,
         solid rations. Front-line endurance with ranged option.
   Body: Ranger chassis (keeps a dog pet option).


6) BEASTMASTER — tame and fight with companions; leash, tripe, charm monster
7) ARTIFICER — wands, charging, devices; gadget toolkit
8) DUELIST — silver saber finesse + haste; single-combat focus
9) ORACLE — detect monsters/treasure, clairvoyance, identify scrolls
10) BERSERKER — battle-axe aggression, minimal gear, max raw power
11) SHADE — daggers, lock pick, displacement cloak, invisibility
12) RUNESMITH — identify spell, enchant scrolls, magic marker
13) PLAGUE DOCTOR — sickness pots, healing, cure sickness
14) TEMPLAR — blessed long sword, shield, turn undead, holy water
15) STORMCALLER — force bolt, cone of cold, lightning wand

Also still available: RANDOM (Rnd) — scrambled stats & gear.

NUM_ROLES is now 29 (13 classic + Random + 15 custom).
Select any from the role list at character creation after rebuild.
